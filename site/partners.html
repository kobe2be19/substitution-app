QUESTIONS

Before you accept.

How much do I earn?

$4 for each coach you refer, for every month they hold a paid subscription, up to 12 months per coach. No percentages and no tiers.

When and how do I get paid?

Monthly, by ACH direct deposit to your US bank account, once the App Store and Google Play pay us for that month. Each payment comes with a statement. Balances under $25 roll over to the next month.

How are referrals tracked?

Your code is an App Store offer code and a Google Play promo code. The stores record each redemption, and we reconcile your coaches against those records every month.

What does the coach get?

Your code may come with an offer for the coach, such as a discount on the first month. We'll tell you the current offer when your code goes live.

Do I need to be a subscriber?

No. Every partner receives a complimentary year of Substitution Pro. Your own subscription doesn't count as a referral.

What about taxes?

You'll sign a W-9 during onboarding, and we issue a Form 1099 where the law requires it.

Is there a cost to join?

No. There's nothing to buy and no minimum number of referrals.

Can I share this page?

Please don't. The program is invitation only. If you know someone who'd be a great partner, send their name to info@substitution.app.
