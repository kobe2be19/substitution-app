Privacy Policy for Substitution

Last updated: September 2026

Substitution does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, on this device or anywhere else.

What the app does with your information

Everything you enter into Substitution — player names, rosters, scores, foul counts, and game settings — stays on your device, in memory, for the length of your session. Closing the app or ending a session clears it. Nothing is uploaded, logged, or sent to any server, because the app has no server: it works entirely offline.

Permissions

The app may request access to vibration and sound on your device, used solely to alert you during a live game (for example, when a player needs to be substituted). No other device permissions are requested, and none are used to collect data.

Third parties

Substitution does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind. No data is shared with third parties because no data leaves your device.

Children's privacy

Substitution does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds optional cloud sync — this page will be updated first, and the date above will reflect that change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: info@bayardelle.com
