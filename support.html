Support

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Substitution is a courtside assistant for basketball coaches: sub calls, minutes, fouls, minimum playing time, game reports, and end-game guidance. It runs on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and is published by Bayardelle & Co. LLC.

Having trouble, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email info@substitution.app and we'll get back to you.

Subscription

Substitution requires a Substitution Pro subscription ($29.99/month, auto-renewing), which unlocks the whole app.

App locked after subscribing, or on a new device? Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in the app's Menu, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel or manage: in the app, open Menu → Manage Subscription. On iPhone or iPad you can also go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, open the Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Refunds: Apple and Google handle payments and refunds. See our Refund Policy for how to request one.

Your support ID

When you email us about your subscription or data, include your support ID: open Menu → Copy Support ID and paste it into your email. It helps us find your purchase without asking for personal details.

Your data

Your rosters, teams, and games are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and deleting the app doesn't cancel your subscription. See our Privacy Policy for details.
